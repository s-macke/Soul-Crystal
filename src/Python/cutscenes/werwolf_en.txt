### werwolf_kampf The fight

## Stories/werwolf/werwolf_kampf-01 pause=1.0
[heroic] Before your undead opponent gets a chance to attack, you lunge at its chest with a deft movement! But just before your blade finds its target, you surprisingly swing it upwards, against the beast's throat, where it leaves a gaping, smoking wound, without the werewolf having the slightest chance of parrying your truly murderous blow! The undead creature, badly wounded by your consecrated weapon, sinks to the ground with a look of disbelief in its eyes and breathes out its "life". [smug] "Knocked out by premature death in the first round!" - smugly you put your sword back into its sheath and blow on your fingernails, when it occurs to you that this could also be a trick: carefully, one hand firmly gripping the hilt of your sword, you kneel down next to the wolf's body to see whether your opponent is really dead... Your worry was unfounded: steaming, greenish blood is running from a long, gaping wound in its throat.

##+ Stories/werwolf/werwolf_kampf-02
[worried] But something else you have to see is a little red blood sticking to one of the wolf's claws - and when you examine yourself, plagued by dark forebodings, you discover a small but rather deep wound on your left leg, which the wolf must have torn while falling, and from which - GREEN blood is now oozing! [shocked] CRAP! Now that you have noticed it, the wound starts to burn like hell: the infection has begun, and it will only be a matter of time before you, too, start turning into a beast at full moon!
