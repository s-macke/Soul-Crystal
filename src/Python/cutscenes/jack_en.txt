### jack_geist Brother Jack's ghost

## Disk 3/DiskB-030
[sad] Desperately, you kneel before the altar

##+ Stories/jack/jack_geist-01
and offer a quick prayer for wittier writing, better graphics, more powerful computers and competent programmers. [dry] You spare a thought for poor Brother Jack, too, who apparently kicked the bucket here. [whispers] After a few lines, though, you get the unpleasant feeling that something is behind you. You turn.

##+ Stories/jack/jack_geist-02
An icy draught stirs the air. Mist is gathering in the chapel, drifting closer and growing denser until it stops right before you... Cold sweat prickles at the back of your neck. Shaking, you stammer, [panicked] "Oh, shit! All right, lads, forget the bit about the writing and everything! Just a joke! Everything's fine, honestly!"

##+ Stories/jack/jack_geist-03
[mysterious] Just before you wet your only pair of trousers, the cloud you had taken for your one-way transport to the hereafter assumes a human shape. There stands [excited] BROTHER JACK!

##+ Stories/jack/jack_geist-04
[warmly] "Peace be with you, my son!" he says. [sly] Then he glances at your trousers, grins, and asks with mock innocence, "Your trousers... ahem. Everything all right?"
