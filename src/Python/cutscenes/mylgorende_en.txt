### mylgor_explosion The explosion

## Stories/mylgor/mylgor_explosion-01
[desperate] In your despair, you tug at one of the massive wooden shelves to hurl the chemicals between you and Mylgor - and it is easier than you expected: one short jerk, and it topples over with everything on it! [surprised] "Oh shit: Arnold!", you think, completely amazed at your sudden strength, as the shelf crashes to the floor and the contents of the various bottles spill over the tiles.

### mylgor_ende Mylgor's end

## Disk 3/DiskB-032 pause=1.0
[hurried] Hastily you lock the laboratory door with the key from Mylgor's study and, to your relief, hear the huge special lock close with a soft click - not a second too soon: at the very moment you turn the key, a terrible explosion in the laboratory shakes the whole house to its foundations - but luckily the massive and wisely locked door keeps the worst of the blast away from you!

##+ Stories/mylgor/mylgor_ende-01
[tense] Plaster trickles from the ceiling, the floor and the walls tremble threateningly - and suddenly everything goes very quiet: only the crackling of the flames licking at the laboratory door can still be heard... When the dust has settled after a while, you look around: the laboratory door is still locked - only black smoke is seeping out of small cracks in its frame... [relieved] You send a prayer of thanks to heaven for having survived the explosion unharmed - [dry] in stark contrast to Mylgor, by the way, who is now probably spread more or less evenly over the entire laboratory... [announcer voice] WELL DONE: BONUS AWARDED! ...NOW PREPARE FOR NEXT STAGE... [embarrassed] Ahem: just kidding...!
