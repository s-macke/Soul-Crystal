### fremde_welt Behind the waterfall

## Disk 2/DiskA-012 pause=1.0
[dazed] Still quite dazed from the hard blow the waterfall gave you, you try to keep yourself above the surface with light but rather clumsy strokes - but suddenly you stop short, so that you go under again for a moment and swallow water!

##+ Stories/fremde_welt/fremde_welt-01
[shocked] When you come up again, it hits you: you still seem to be paddling on the southern side of the waterfall! There might even be some plausible explanation for that alone, especially since you lost your bearings under water for a moment, so you could accept it if, if... if only your village were still there on the shore! [panicked] But it seems to have vanished from the face of the earth! Instead of the little village, there is a deep, dark forest on the shore, with trees you have never seen in your whole life! [thoughtful] You begin to suspect that the waterfall may have taken you into a world that exists parallel to your own - just like in those silly adventure games on your computer at home. [dramatic] Only: THIS is reality! And where the hell have you ended up?
