## Stories/sage/sage-01
[cheerful] You wave the waitress over to you and tell her your most urgent wishes: "Please bring me a large steak, a cold beer and the Fra..." - [embarrassed] Suddenly you feel yourself blushing and stammer: "Oops: forget the Frankfurter Allgemeine. You probably don't get any German newspapers here anyway, right?" [smug] When the girl, also red up to her ears, hastily says no, you reassure her - deliberately ignoring the age difference of a good ten years - in a fatherly way: "It doesn't matter, my daughter: I can't speak German anyway!", [laughs] and burst into a roar of laughter, [dry] whereupon the villager, who had been sitting there uninvolved, rolls his eyes up to the sky in suffering, as if he had been kicked in the soft parts where it really hurts. When you realize with dismay that the waitress also appears to be suffering from a chronic lack of humor, you decide to abruptly stop your comic performance and dismiss the girl with a merciful wave of your hand.

[mumbles] Slowly and haltingly, the villager begins to mumble into his beard that he can tell you an interesting legend. [sarcastic] Well, you didn't exactly expect him to tell you the addresses of the local hot spots and yuppie discos, but does it have to be a fairy tale? [sighs] As a precaution, you rest your head on your hands so that in an emergency you don't hit your nose on the edge of the table, and while you wait longingly for your food, so that you'd at least have something to do, you listen to the old man's story:
