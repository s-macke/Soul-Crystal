Here are the exits. West leads to a small gatehouse, where you might find a last-minute clue or useful object. North takes you into the ruins, or headfirst into solid oak, depending on whether the gate is open. You could also retreat south, away from the castle, though that won't do much to complete your mission, will it?

## 224

Hmm... I know some people swear by mud baths, but this is a deep swamp, not a harmless spa treatment! You sink helplessly to the bottom of the lake, where you'll be preserved for all eternity. Personally, I think preserving yourself with the "Save" command would have been rather healthier...

## 225

After a few paces, you stop and listen to the deceptive silence. No, you weren't imagining it. Something is moving beneath the constant bubbling of the bog. Footsteps approach from behind, slow but relentless. You whirl round, but see only thick mist twisting into grotesque faces that seem to mock your fear. Suddenly the ground beside you begins to boil. A horribly disfigured face breaks the surface: a half-rotted corpse! Frozen with horror, you watch a zombie drag itself from the depths. As you regain control of your limbs, its claws shoot towards your throat! Somehow you fling yourself aside in time, but stumble and land hard in the mud. The undead creature seems to have won. While you struggle to your feet, it staggers closer, hissing and snarling. Its jaws creak open and shut, exposing the revolting brown stumps of its teeth...

## 226

Sorry. You had a real chance to escape with nothing worse than a fright, but you hesitated too long. The delighted zombie pulls you close and sinks its teeth into your young, tender flesh! Still, don't despair. You've fallen victim to a true gourmet. Some of your tastier parts, eyes, tongue and similar delicacies, will doubtless be taken to its store-cave and preserved. Unfortunate adventurers stray out here far too rarely to waste such a feast in one sitting...

## 227

Coming back after your last encounter was a fatal mistake. A whole pack of zombies waits to welcome you, advancing with jerky movements and fixed stares. Leading them is the one you only just escaped! This time you react much faster. You spin round and run for your life as fast as the soggy ground allows, straight into the arms of another zombie who hadn't quite caught up with the proceedings. A shame, really. You were on course for a world record! Your new acquaintance is delighted. It hasn't had fresh meat in weeks, and a diet of bog-preserved adventurers gets a bit monotonous, even for a half-rotten stomach. After saying a quick grace, it gratefully sinks its teeth into you. "Damn! Who knew those brown stumps could still be so sharp?" is your last thought before everything blurs...

## 228

As you head south out of the enchanted forest, the amulet that protected you on your way through the magical barrier snags on a dead branch. Without your noticing, it slips from your neck. You stroll cheerfully through the boundary between the two stretches of forest and emerge on the other side of the magical wall nicely browned, but unfortunately quite dead...

## 229

Walking beneath the dead trees, you suddenly feel a vicious tug at your neck! Terrified, you thrash about, trying to shake off your invisible attacker. At last it lets go, and you nearly burst into hysterical laughter. Your "attacker" was your own amulet, caught on a branch. It now dangles from the tree as if mocking you...

## 230

Well, let's call that an attempt and leave it there. It wasn't much of a success, as you discover rather painfully. Before you can slip beneath the archway, something rattles down from above. For once, let's keep this brief: stepping on the little platform triggered the portcullis, which crushed you in one almighty blow! You're dead. Yes, really. You've kicked the bucket, bitten the dust, shuffled off this mortal coil. Gone! Don't complain to us. If you'd paid attention, you might have noticed the platform beneath the gate. So having to start again is rather more your fault than ours, isn't it? What's that? You don't believe me? Well, I can't say I blame you. You have no reason to. Perhaps this will convince you...:

## 231

## 232

## 233

A brick is a brick is a brick, and this disk is a broken record, broken record, broken rec... Oh, COME ON! What did you expect from a BRICK?!?!? Small, square, perfectly adequate. Happy now?

## 234

## 235

## 236

## 237

The little platform seems to be set into the ground to trigger something. A bell? A Mark III flying-toaster catapult? A high-speed disk formatter? Or, even sillier, a TRAP? No, surely that would be too unimaginative even for this game!

## 238

YUCK! Looking at the zombie, you struggle not to throw up straight into its rotting face. It's about shoulder-height, and every tiny movement sends scraps of putrid flesh dropping from its bones...

## 239

As you try to free the amulet from the branch, it slips through your still-trembling fingers, falls to the ground and vanishes into the mud with a little "Plop!"...

## 240

The hermit gratefully accepts your offer and asks you to clear away the junk outside his hut. "My poor back, you understand...?" Personally, I think we should let the old hypochondriac do it himself. A little fresh air and exercise will do him good. You have more important things to do than play dustman...

## 241

The zombie would be grateful for the rest of your life if you gave it an eye. You do have two, after all, and it hasn't had a decent bite to eat all day...

## 242

With a great effort, you pull the portcullis's points out of the ground and heave it upwards until it jams in its frame. The danger seems to be over for now. You should be able to slip underneath it and through the archway...

## 243

Years of solitude seem to have scrambled the old man's brains, but you ask what he knows about the island anyway. "The island, the island..." he muses. "If only I knew which one you meant! The island Aeneas, that divine hero, visited long ago? Ah, I've heard of an island called America, discovered by a fellow named Columbo. No, Coulomb. Rubbish, Columbos! Oh, never mind. Nobody cares anyway. Or do you mean the island we're standing on? Yes, this is an island too, believe it or not! You can tell because it's surrounded by water, and..." You roll your eyes. This advanced geography lesson is bringing back unpleasant memories of Sesame Street. You didn't understand it then, either...

## 244

With saintly patience, you give the old man's rambling another chance, though you doubt it will be any more enlightening this time. Surprisingly, he almost answers your question. "Yes, of course I know about the island! I've lived here for over ten years! Quite right to ask me. I live here, you see, and..." He falters as he notices your hands practising a variety of strangling movements, then hastily continues. "Now, you may think me mad" - you laugh hysterically - "but at midnight the rivers here flow uphill! And under a full moon, all the adventurers swallowed by the swamp over the decades rise from their watery graves and hold wild orgies! Even Zargon, the Dark Lord himself, attends to review his undead palace guard. He once made a pact with the devil, who had his soul frozen and imprisoned in a huge magical crystal..." A knowing smile spreads over the hermit's wrinkled face as he remembers those glorious parties and the many pretty zombie girls he's enjoyed them with...

## 245

You might want to hear more of the old man's mindless rambling, but I DON'T! Honestly, even THIS nonsense is bearable by comparison...

## 246

Suspecting one of the unimaginative traps that infest this country, you carefully drop the poor, innocent brick onto the platform from a safe distance. The instant it lands, the enormous portcullis crashes down! Not even a rat could have darted through in time. The brick becomes a thousand convenient little pebbles. Your heart clenches and drops into your stomach. What would you look like if YOUR weight had triggered that mechanism? More to the point, who cleaned up the mess left by the adventurers before you???

## 247

You put the big iron key into the enormous lock and turn it. Unfortunately, you snap it off! Where did THAT strength come from? Still, at least the castle gate is unlocked now...

## 248
