THE STORY

[Page 1]

"Here at last! Wasnt
as hard as i expected
at first!", you think
to yourself as you
step out of the bus that has taken you
from the station of the neighbouring
village to your hotel. The first thing
you do is breathe a sigh of relief:
you somehow managed to escape the
annoying advice of your parents for the
next days and weeks - something you
never dreamed possible...

Until one day, on the
verge of a nervous
breakdown - you moved
out of the house...

[Page 2]

As you stroll down the street,
you think back about your departure,
to your very worried parents who even
thought they had to
give you advice
through the passenger
window at the train
station - and once
again got on your
nerves with their
constant talking and
their limitless pessimism...  "What do
they want from me??? I am seventeen
after all, that is almost old enough to
marry! - why shouldnt I be able to spend
my holidays alone?"

Seventeen years - and
you spent thirteen of
them, every year, at
the the same time,
in the same old
french town,

[Page 3]

with your parents, who - out of a pure
sense of responsibility, of course -
dont want to miss any opportunity to
monitor every single step you take!

"But now ive had
enough of it!", you
said to yourself one
day, and in your
usual courtesy,
informed them that
you refuse to spend
another holiday with
them among all these apparently dead
people in this french "resort" town...

You can still remember the horrified
faces of your parents all too well,
when their son actually dared speak-up.
You can still hear your fathers bogus
arguments in your head, which he
articulated so well in all the endless
discussions...

[Page 4]

Especially in the
last two months, the
arguments seemed to
never end. At least
you were able to
utimately prevail!


Well you have to admit, with a slight
smile on your face, that you have
actually been playing with your thoughts
over the last few weeks, sitting at home
while while your parents were out
partying. And one night you finally
realized, you are almost forced to
travel - if only to show your parents
whos the boss in the house!

Well, now you are finally here!

You take a deep breath of fresh clean
air and look around: YES!

[Page 5]

This is exactly the right place for
young geniuses like you to be able to
recover from your stressful everyday
lives!

The adult life! You
are completely free -
although only for a
couple of weeks, but
still free - and that
is the only thing
that matters to you at the moment. Now
you can do what you want, wear what you
want, and last but not least, drink as
much as you want!!

And, well, when your
vacation is over
hopefully you will be
recovered and strong
enough to be able to
survive at home for a
few months until you
turn eighteen...

[Page 6]

Happy and full of the
energy that you used
to have when you were
in school, and that
isnt normal now, you
enter your hotel,
sign the guestbook,
and receive your room
key. You drag your luggage to your room
and start unpacking, when you happen to
look at your wristwatch. Its already
half-past six and you
wanted to get out and
have a look at the
"Loch Calderwood".

The old man you met
on the bus ride had
so many legendary
stories to tell about it.

So you leave your things as they are for
now and lock your room door...

[Page 7]

...behind you and leave the key at the
reception desk so you
will be able to take
a quick look at the
landscape before its
time for dinner.
After all, you want
to be able to tell
your mother some
stories when you arrive back home later.

It would be even nicer if she has been
worried the whole time, that her little
boy may have taken the wrong bus and is
now wandering, lonely and abandoned,
through Scotlands forests...

[Page 8]

When you step out of
the hotel lobby and
back on to the dusty
street, to go search
for the legendary
mountain lake, you
can feel the warm,
red-golden light of the setting sun on
your face...
