The Diary of the Tower Guardian

1st Day of the Year 713:

This morning the couriers finally returned from the frozen lands. They had visited Firon, our king's brother, in his palace to deliver the king's blessings for the new year. Yet none of them looked pleased or hopeful. Quite the opposite: some message from Firon, the Elven King, must have frightened them badly. I have been unable to discover more, but even King Richard looked deeply troubled as he left his council chamber with the envoys.

2nd Day of the Year 713:

The new year begins with an ill omen. The noble Sir Henryk was killed on today's hunt when his horse stumbled during the pursuit of a badger and fell on top of him. Apart from the old Archmage Grywinn and the king himself, Sir Henryk was the last person at court to oppose admitting the young, ambitious court magician Zargon to the Seventh Circle of the Mages' Guild. Now the king and Grywinn stand alone against the other three archmages, and Zargon is close to his goal.

For years he has shut himself away to perfect his knowledge of the magical elements, but does he also possess the strength of character and wisdom needed to use that knowledge for the good of our people? We do not even know where he comes from. He has never spoken to any of us about himself. He is secretive and mysterious, and something about him frightens me.

3rd Day of the Year 713:

Early this morning, the nobles of the realm laid Sir Henryk to rest in the graveyard of St Jacobi's monastery. After they returned from the funeral service, a great banquet was held in his honour. To take my mind off these dark thoughts, I looked down from my chamber in the main tower at the young officers strolling through the courtyard with their sweethearts. Servants hurried to and fro, bringing this guest one thing and that guest another, determined that the king's company should want for nothing. When Vyrton relieved me for an hour, I went down to join them.

No one noticed a lowly tower guard among all those finely dressed guests, so I could watch undisturbed. Then a young woman hurried past, so beautiful and graceful that, but for her serving apron, I would have taken her for a noblewoman. I must still have been standing there thunderstruck when she came back. She blushed, gave me a cheerful wink and vanished into the kitchen. After this had happened two or three times, I finally plucked up the courage to catch her arm and ask her name. "Simona," she said.

There was no time to talk. She had guests to attend to, and my break was over. But before I left, Simona whispered to me that she had put aside a few treats from the feast. If I wished, I could meet her in the castle basement just before midnight, and we could have a little feast of our own. Now I sit here looking down into the courtyard and counting the minutes. My only consolation is that I can admire Simona from my window as much as I please. She has just spotted me and waved up, laughing. It seems she rather likes me too. I do hope she comes tonight!

Simona did not disappoint me. Just before midnight, she slipped into the basement so quietly that I jumped when she suddenly appeared before me. I was mortified, but she merely smiled and pointed conspiratorially to a basket of delicacies rescued from the banquet. Over good food and a carafe of rich wine, we grew ever closer — whether through the wine's charms or fate's designs, I cannot say. At last, emboldened by the wine, I confessed my love. She had just begun to say the words I had longed to hear since our first meeting when we heard soft footsteps on the stone spiral staircase leading to the entrance hall.

To protect her reputation, I drew her into a recess beyond the torchlight and hid beside her to see who had dared interrupt our secret meeting. It was Zargon! He crept past us and along the corridor, stopping at a heavy door. After a hurried glance over his shoulder, he pushed the door handle upwards and disappeared into the darkness beyond.

Still shaken by this strange interruption, I wondered why he would be studying tonight of all nights. Tomorrow he would enter the Seventh Circle of the Guild and fulfil his greatest ambition. Surely he ought to be asleep, ready to face his great day with a clear head. But what did we care about Zargon and his ridiculous conjuring tricks? There were more important things to attend to...

4th Day of the Year 713:

Yesterday ended so beautifully. Today was terrible. Before Zargon's initiation, Grywinn cast the rune-sticks to seek the gods' judgement on his elevation to archmage. Suddenly, a bolt of lightning struck from a clear sky and reduced Grywinn and his rune-sticks to ashes. Screams broke out. People fled in every direction. Only Zargon remained calm.

Standing tall beside Grywinn's charred body, he took the pentagram amulet intended for his initiation and hung it around his own neck. "If the most powerful magician in the land can no longer appoint me, then the second most powerful will have to do it!" he cried.

The king and the terrified crowd were too stunned by the old mage's death to protest. Richard alone looked at Zargon with open contempt, making it plain that he knew who was responsible. I saw Grywinn's face just before the lightning struck. Whatever he had seen in the future had filled him with terror...

5th Day of the Year 713:

I am afraid. I can scarcely sleep for fear of what Grywinn foresaw. Now that Zargon can wield the full extent of his magic, will he turn it against his own people? Yesterday proved that he will kill to gain power. What terrible deed can he be preparing, if even its preparation demands a murder? Woe to us if my fears prove true!

Zargon is seen less and less. When he does appear, it is often in the gloomy vaults where the king's ancestors lie buried — a place none of us ordinary servants would dare enter alone. If he notices anyone else there, he hurries out without a word, almost as though fleeing something.

While I sat with Simona in the castle kitchen during my midday break, we could see into the dining hall. Zargon was arguing fiercely with King Richard. He wanted the army taught the rudiments of magic to make it stronger, a proposal Richard had rejected years ago. The power-hungry magician seemed to have lost all respect for his ruler's decisions. He even called Richard a coward who was not man enough to contemplate war. At last the king rose in anger and strode out. Zargon remained at the table, smiling to himself and toying with his dinner knife...

6th Day of the Year 713:

Something dreadful has happened! Noble warrior, if you should one day read these words, know that we are in mortal danger. Only the almighty gods can save our poor souls now. At the stroke of midnight, I heard shouting in the courtyard and hurried to the window. Horror seized me. Four half-rotted mummies were staggering across the courtyard to open the great castle gate. It had barely opened a crack before a vast army of hideously disfigured monsters poured in and seized the castle.

Screams and cries filled the halls as the raging horde cut down everyone who resisted. Then all fell silent. A small band of rotting corpses, raised into a hideous mockery of life, emerged from the main building. In their midst was King Richard. He offered no resistance as they dragged him into another building. A few seconds of silence — then my lord's long, agonised death cry!

May the gods have mercy on my soul. The king's surviving soldiers have been herded into the courtyard like cattle for slaughter. They huddle together, trembling like frightened children. Now Zargon emerges from the doorway through which we heard our master's final cry: that wretched traitor, murderer and blasphemer! Slowly he walks along the line of prisoners, then steps onto a ledge. He offers the murdered king's followers a choice: serve Zargon as their rightful lord, or follow their king in death.

Not one man raises a hand to join him. Zargon unleashes his pack on the defenceless prisoners. Within moments, the courtyard becomes a slaughterhouse. None of them survives...

Zargon has just ordered his henchmen to search the castle for anyone still alive. It is only a matter of time before they find me up here and drag me down.
